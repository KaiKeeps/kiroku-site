Every +rep goes on the record.

Kiroku (記録, “record”) is a reputation bot for Discord. Thank someone with +rep, call them out with -rep, and it stamps the new total where everyone can see it.

How rep works

Three rules. You already know the first one.

Give it or take it

Type +rep @user or -rep @user, or reply to a message with just +rep or -rep. Any case works. Add a reason if you like.

Kiroku answers with the new total and pings only the person who got it.

One cooldown per person

After you rep someone, you wait before repping them again: an hour, unless the server’s admins set it differently. Everyone else is fair game, back to back.

Too soon for the same person? You get a ⏳ and nothing else.

No repping yourself. Or bots.

Self-rep gets a 🚫 and one of a few lines: “You can’t rep yourself lol”, “Nice try. 🙃”, or the one above. Rep aimed at a bot gets the 🚫 and no reply, Kiroku included.

Four slash commands

Rep happens in plain chat. These are for looking things up.

/replist
The leaderboard. Medals for the top three, numbered tags after that, 50 members a page in two columns of 25. Your own rank sits at the bottom of every page.
/replist-
The same board flipped: most negative first, under a 👎.
/profile [member]
A profile card as an image. Yours, or anyone’s you name.
/daily
Once every 24 hours, a 25% shot at a Rep Again.

Admins get a fifth: /cooldown sets how long you wait before repping the same person again. One minute for chaos, twenty-four hours for grudges.

Shortened to fit. Further down the board, the same footer might read “You: #237 — -2 rep”.

Rep Again skips the wait

A Rep Again lets you rep someone you’re still on cooldown for.

Rep Again

Good for one rep, cooldown or not.

Announced in chat, once

The announcement is its own message, right after the usual reply. No box, no fanfare, one line.

  • Each stamp is announced once per member, ever. Dip back under and climb again, and Kiroku stays quiet.
  • It pings the member who earned it and nobody else.
  • Every stamp has a couple of lines and Kiroku picks one. At 1,000 you might get “🏆 Four digits!”

Your profile card

/profile draws your card as an image and posts it. Name a member to pull up theirs.

  • Avatar, display name, @username, and the highest title you’ve reached.
  • Your rep, your rank, and how many Rep Agains you’re holding.
  • Your top five fans, the people who’ve given you the most +rep.
  • Your top three stans, the ones who keep giving you -rep. They care, in their way.

It talks back

Mention Kiroku and it answers with whatever it feels like. Mention it again inside half a minute and it ignores you.

It knows when to be quiet

Admins can close channels to Kiroku. In those, +rep does nothing at all, and a slash command gets a private “Kiroku doesn’t work in this channel.” If nothing happens, try a different channel.

  • # general
  • # clips
  • # late-night
  • # announcements quiet
  • # rules quiet
